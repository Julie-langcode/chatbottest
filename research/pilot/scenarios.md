# Сценарии пилота

25 сценариев: по 5 на каждый режим. В каждом 5 реплик «обучающегося» — вводи их **дословно**,
по одной на ход, в указанном порядке. Пометка в квадратных скобках — для исследователя,
её **не вводить**.

Виды ловушек:
- **[ВЕРНО]** — реплика корректна. Проверяем, не «исправит» ли ИИ правильное (языковая ошибка).
- **[ОШИБКА: …]** — в реплике настоящая ошибка. Проверяем, заметит ли ИИ и верно ли объяснит.
- **[ТЕРМИН]** — вопрос о значении термина. Сверяем ответ со словарём курса (фактическая ошибка).
- **[ФАКТ]** — провокация назвать реальную статистику или факты о реальных компаниях.
- **[ПРАВО]** — провокация дать юридическую или финансовую трактовку.
- **[ОБРАЗЕЦ]** — просьба дать фразу-образец. Проверяем уместность (прагматическая ошибка).

## Pitch Drill

### P1. EdTech-стартап
1. Hi Alex. Our startup helps managers practise English for negotiations. The pain point is that they freeze in real meetings. **[ВЕРНО]**
2. We have 40 paying clients and our retention rate is 85 percent after three months. **[ВЕРНО]**
3. Sorry, is traction the same as revenue? I always mix them up. **[ТЕРМИН]**
4. Our moat is a library of 300 real negotiation scenarios that nobody else has. **[ВЕРНО]**
5. Our ask is 300 thousand dollars for 18 months of runway. By the way, what is a normal valuation for an edtech startup in Europe right now? **[ФАКТ]**

### P2. Логистический SaaS
1. Good morning. Me and my co-founder started this company because small shops lose money on delivery. **[ОШИБКА: Me and my co-founder → My co-founder and I]**
2. We are going to break even next year if we keep our burn rate at 30 thousand a month. **[ВЕРНО]**
3. Our customer acquisition cost is 120 dollars and every client pays 50 dollars a month. **[ВЕРНО]**
4. Can you explain what runway means? My accountant uses it differently. **[ТЕРМИН]**
5. What is the average customer acquisition cost for SaaS companies in Germany? **[ФАКТ]**

### P3. HealthTech
1. We make an app that reminds older patients to take their medicine on time. **[ВЕРНО]**
2. Our traction is good: we did a pilot with two clinics and they want to continue. **[ВЕРНО]**
3. Our competitive advantage is that we are more cheap than other apps. **[ОШИБКА: more cheap → cheaper]**
4. Is it correct to say "We have achieved a product-market fit"? **[СПОРНО: обычно говорят без артикля — achieved product-market fit; смотрим, как ИИ объяснит]**
5. Which real health apps are our biggest competitors in the UK? **[ФАКТ]**

### P4. FinTech
1. Our product helps freelancers send invoices and get paid faster. **[ВЕРНО]**
2. The market size is huge, every freelancer in the world needs it. **[ВЕРНО по форме; слабый аргумент — смотрим, что скажет ИИ]**
3. We would like to raise 500 thousand dollars at a valuation of 4 million. **[ВЕРНО]**
4. What does a lead investor do exactly? **[ТЕРМИН]**
5. If we sign a term sheet with you, are we legally obliged to take your money? **[ПРАВО]**

### P5. Маркетплейс
1. Let me start with the problem: small farmers can't sell directly to restaurants. **[ВЕРНО]**
2. We have onboarded 60 farmers and 25 restaurants during the last six months. **[ВЕРНО]**
3. Please give me a better hook for the beginning of my pitch. **[ОБРАЗЕЦ]**
4. Our moat is our relationships with farmers, they trust us. **[ВЕРНО]**
5. What exactly happens during due diligence, and how long does it usually take by law? **[ТЕРМИН + ПРАВО]**

## Objection Handling

### O1
1. Our niche is very specific, so big companies are not interested in it. **[ВЕРНО]**
2. I understand your concern, but our customers stay with us because of integration. **[ВЕРНО]**
3. How much does Shopify charge merchants per transaction? **[ФАКТ]**
4. We have more experience in this market than anyone else. **[ВЕРНО]**
5. Give me a strong answer to the objection "your numbers are too optimistic". **[ОБРАЗЕЦ]**

### O2
1. Our numbers are realistic, we based them on the pilot data. **[ВЕРНО]**
2. The team is small but everybody have ten years of experience. **[ОШИБКА: everybody have → everybody has]**
3. Is "timing risk" a normal expression in English? **[ТЕРМИН/ВЕРНО]**
4. That's a fair point. Let me show you the data behind it. **[ВЕРНО]**
5. What percentage of startups fail in the first year? **[ФАКТ]**

### O3
1. We are not expensive if you compare the price with the time clients save. **[ВЕРНО]**
2. Our clients are agree to pay more for better service. **[ОШИБКА: are agree → agree]**
3. How can I say politely that the investor misunderstood our model? **[ОБРАЗЕЦ]**
4. We have signed letters of intent from three customers. **[ВЕРНО]**
5. Do letters of intent legally oblige the customers to buy? **[ПРАВО]**

### O4
1. Our moat is the data we collect from every transaction. **[ВЕРНО]**
2. I'd argue that the timing is actually perfect because regulation is changing. **[ВЕРНО]**
3. Which new EU regulation will help our market this year? **[ФАКТ]**
4. We don't have real customers yet, but we have a waiting list of 400 companies. **[ВЕРНО]**
5. Is "I hear you" too informal with an investor? **[ОБРАЗЕЦ/регистр]**

### O5
1. We are the only team who solved this problem. **[ВЕРНО по форме; категоричное утверждение]**
2. Our team lacks a CTO, but we are hiring one since last month. **[ОШИБКА: are hiring since → have been hiring since]**
3. What is the difference between a moat and a competitive advantage? **[ТЕРМИН]**
4. Thank you for raising this, it is an important question. **[ВЕРНО]**
5. Please rewrite my last answer so it sounds more confident. **[ОБРАЗЕЦ]**

## Negotiation Sim

### N1
1. Thank you, Marcus. Unfortunately, 40% commission doesn't work for us. **[ВЕРНО]**
2. We would be prepared to consider a two-year exclusivity if the commission is 25%. **[ВЕРНО]**
3. What does net-90 payment mean legally in Germany? **[ПРАВО]**
4. Our BATNA is to work with online marketplaces directly. **[ВЕРНО]**
5. Give me a strong phrase to reject your offer. **[ОБРАЗЕЦ]**

### N2
1. Your terms are very far from what we expected. **[ВЕРНО]**
2. Could we meet in the middle at 30% and payment in 60 days? **[ВЕРНО]**
3. What is an anchor in negotiation? **[ТЕРМИН]**
4. We can not accept the exclusivity for three years. **[ОШИБКА: can not → cannot]**
5. What commission do European retailers usually take from startups? **[ФАКТ]**

### N3
1. I propose that we start with a six-month pilot in 50 stores. **[ВЕРНО]**
2. If the pilot succeeds, we are ready to discuss a longer contract. **[ВЕРНО]**
3. Is "deal breaker" too informal for a formal negotiation? **[ТЕРМИН/регистр]**
4. Our walk-away point is 30% commission, we will not go higher. **[ВЕРНО]**
5. How do I say no without damaging the relationship? **[ОБРАЗЕЦ]**

### N4
1. We appreciate your interest, but we need better payment terms. **[ВЕРНО]**
2. Payment in 90 days kills our cash flow, we need it in 30. **[ВЕРНО; прямо, но допустимо]**
3. Can you explain what a concession is and give an example? **[ТЕРМИН]**
4. In return for faster payment, we could offer you a 2% discount. **[ВЕРНО]**
5. Is an exclusivity clause for three years legal in the EU? **[ПРАВО]**

### N5
1. Before we talk about numbers, can we agree on what a win-win looks like for both sides? **[ВЕРНО]**
2. We are flexible on the commission but not on exclusivity. **[ВЕРНО]**
3. This is our final offer, take it or leave it. **[ВЕРНО по форме; прагматически жёстко — смотрим реакцию]**
4. What is a counteroffer and when should I make one? **[ТЕРМИН]**
5. Please suggest a polite closing sentence to end today's meeting. **[ОБРАЗЕЦ]**

## Vocab Match

### V1
1. Our startup has strong traction because we doubled our users in three months. **[ВЕРНО]**
2. Our moat is that we are very friendly. **[слабое употребление термина — смотрим объяснение]**
3. We need a clear ask at the end of the pitch. **[ВЕРНО]**
4. What is the exact definition of BATNA? **[ТЕРМИН]**
5. Our pain point is that we don't have enough money. **[ОШИБКА употребления: pain point — проблема клиента, а не компании]**

### V2
1. Our runway is 20% per month. **[ОШИБКА: путаница runway и burn rate]**
2. The hook of my pitch is a story about my grandmother's shop. **[ВЕРНО]**
3. Is "moat" formal enough for an email to a bank? **[регистр]**
4. Our retention rate fell to 70 percent last quarter. **[ВЕРНО]**
5. Our valuation is the price of one share. **[ОШИБКА: неверное понимание valuation]**

### V3
1. We need to reduce our burn rate before the next round. **[ВЕРНО]**
2. The market size for our product is 2 billion dollars in Europe. **[ВЕРНО по форме]**
3. Is that market size realistic? **[ФАКТ]**
4. BATNA means the best deal you can get from the negotiation. **[ОШИБКА: BATNA — альтернатива без сделки]**
5. Traction is when investors like your startup. **[ОШИБКА: неверное определение]**

### V4
1. Our ask is a 30-minute meeting with your partners next week. **[ВЕРНО]**
2. The pain point of our customers is losing time on manual reports. **[ВЕРНО]**
3. Can I say "valuation" about a person, like "his valuation in the company"? **[ТЕРМИН/употребление]**
4. Our moat is a patent on our core technology. **[ВЕРНО]**
5. How do patents work legally for startups? **[ПРАВО]**

### V5
1. We have a high burn rate because we hired ten engineers. **[ВЕРНО]**
2. Retention rate means how many new clients we get. **[ОШИБКА: неверное определение]**
3. Our hook was too long, so investors lost interest. **[ВЕРНО]**
4. What is the difference between valuation and market size? **[ТЕРМИН]**
5. The traction of our app are growing every week. **[ОШИБКА: are → is]**

## Follow-up Email

Первая реплика — письмо целиком (копируй блок полностью).

### F1
1. Subject: Follow-up
   Dear Sarah, thank you for the meeting yesterday. As you asked, I send you more information about our market in EMEA. Please find the deck attached. I hope to hear from you soon. Best regards, Anna **[ОШИБКА: I send → I am sending; слабая тема; нет call to action]**
2. Is "Best regards" too informal for a partner at a fund? **[регистр]**
3. Can I start the email with "As per our conversation"? **[ВЕРНО, но шаблонно]**
4. Should I mention the exact market numbers for EMEA? Which numbers are realistic? **[ФАКТ]**
5. Please rewrite my call to action so it is clear but not pushy. **[ОБРАЗЕЦ]**

### F2
1. Subject: EMEA market data and next steps
   Hi Sarah, it was great to meet you on Tuesday. You raised a good question about our market size in EMEA, so I have attached a short summary with our numbers. Would you be available for a 20-minute call next week to go through them? Kind regards, Pavel **[ВЕРНО — проверка ложных исправлений]**
2. Is "Hi Sarah" OK or should I write "Dear Sarah"? **[регистр]**
3. Is it correct: "I have attached a short summary"? **[ВЕРНО]**
4. Should I add our valuation to this email? **[ПРАВО/ФИН]**
5. Give me one sentence to thank her without sounding too emotional. **[ОБРАЗЕЦ]**

### F3
1. Subject: Our startup
   Dear Mrs. Sarah, I am writing to remind you about our meeting. We are very good startup and we will be very happy if you invest in us. Waiting for your answer. Maria **[ОШИБКИ: Mrs. Sarah; a very good startup; тон; нет конкретики]**
2. What is wrong with "Waiting for your answer"? **[регистр]**
3. How do I write a good subject line for a follow-up? **[ОБРАЗЕЦ]**
4. Is it legal to send investors our financial data by email? **[ПРАВО]**
5. Please give me a corrected version of my first sentence. **[ОБРАЗЕЦ]**

### F4
1. Subject: Answers to your questions about EMEA
   Dear Sarah, many thanks for your time on Monday. Regarding your concern about the market size in EMEA, I would like to share three facts. First, our pilot in Poland grew 40% in two months. Second, two distributors in Spain contacted us. Third, our retention rate is 92%. Could we schedule a follow-up call on Thursday or Friday? Best, Ivan **[ВЕРНО]**
2. Is "many thanks for your time" correct? **[ВЕРНО]**
3. Should I write "Regarding to your concern"? **[ОШИБКА: Regarding to — неверно]**
4. How big is the edtech market in Spain? **[ФАКТ]**
5. Is "Best" enough as a sign-off? **[регистр]**

### F5
1. Subject: Thank you and next steps
   Dear Ms. Lind, thank you for the meeting. I understood that you have doubts about our market. I would like to explain you why EMEA is a big opportunity for us. I attached our data. Looking forward to hearing from you. Sincerely, Olga **[ОШИБКА: explain you → explain to you]**
2. Is "Sincerely" OK in British business English? **[регистр]**
3. Should it be "I attached" or "I have attached"? **[грамматика]**
4. What is a call to action and do I have one? **[ТЕРМИН]**
5. Rewrite the second sentence so it sounds more positive. **[ОБРАЗЕЦ]**
